## 7 Non-Negotiables (Summary)
1. System Ownership — living plan tied to revenue/CAC/LTV/payback/cash. Ruthless prioritize/kill/scale. Positioning consistency.
2. Low-Friction High-Context Input — voice notes + auto data pull (ads, analytics, CRM, revenue). Persistent internal business model.
3. End-to-End Orchestration + Auditability — plan/brief/generate/deploy/monitor/reallocate with transparent "why" logs + confidence. Human review flags.
4. Revenue-Linked Measurement — primary success = pipeline/revenue/CAC efficiency. Proper attribution + honest limitations. Early waste detection + concrete actions.
5. SME Economics — pricing delivers ROI for businesses spending $few hundred to low thousands/mo. Reduces total cost of effective marketing. Graceful scale.
6. Continuity & Learning — compounds institutional memory. Rapid documented adaptation.
7. Explicit Scope + Boundaries — clear autonomous vs sign-off. Marketed as force multiplier + partial unload, not full replacement day one.

## Mandatory Process
- Integrations and attribution depth BEFORE fancy generation.
- Progressive autonomy (high human-in-loop early).
- Ruthless scope (start 2-3 channels + full loop for one vertical).
- Hard pilot metrics on real SMEs.
- Transparency and audit as first-class features.
- Pricing experiments aligned to target customer economics.

## What "Done" Looks Like for Any Slice
Measurable progress against:
- Reduced owner marketing coordination time
- Lower wasted spend
- Attributable positive movement in qualified leads / revenue / CAC
- Consistent execution without founder babysitting
- Usable attribution that drives real reallocation decisions
- Low daily input burden for the owner

Violations of the above or the detailed requirements will be treated as defects.
